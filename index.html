Choosing your sport
Tips on practicing and techniques
How to stay on focus
  • Choose a sport that works well with your personality and aligns with your socialization skills
  • Consider your own physical health and fitness
  • Check the limitations and the practicality of you practice
  • When practicing, make sure to consider your physical skills and limitations
  • Break down and analyze the the skillset given by the specific sport, when comparing these skills to yours, it is best to ask for advice and feedback from a coach
  • To focus, make sure to secure your strategies and skills
  • Focus on the current moment than what will come after
  • Practice breath excersises, in order to keep relaxation and focus on the game
